# Consent to be recorded

This playtest is for Playtest Lens, a hackathon project by Sasha. Please read this before you start, and reply "I agree" (and your choice below) when you send your recording.

**What's recorded:** your screen while you play, your voice through the microphone, and a log of what you did in the game (buttons pressed, places visited). Your face is not recorded.

**What it's used for:** testing the tool, which analyzes playtests with AI models through Meta's API (the recording is sent there for analysis). The results help build and judge the project.

**Who sees it:** Sasha, and the AI models that analyze it. It is not published unless you choose to allow that below.

**Your choice, pick one:**
- **Private:** my recording is used only for testing and is never shown publicly.
- **Public:** a short part of my recording, with my voice, may appear in the project's public demo video.

**Keeping and deleting:** recordings are stored privately, never in the public code repository, and deleted after the hackathon's judging (by the end of January 2027) unless you allowed public use. You can ask Sasha to delete your recording at any time, and he will.
